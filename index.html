Research

Peer-reviewed work on fault tolerance, reliability, and cost/availability tradeoffs in networked control systems.

M.S. Thesis · The American University in Cairo · 2022

Fault Tolerance for Access Point Failures in Smart Greenhouse Networked Control Systems

Designed and simulated a fault-tolerant wireless-sensor-network control architecture in Riverbed Modeler that keeps operating through single and double access-point failures, then quantified the tradeoff between the cost of redundant infrastructure and the financial impact of downtime.
